Preprint · 2026

DualOPSD: Adaptive Privileged Teachers for On-Policy Self-Distillation

Yutong Chen, Guangfu Guo, Zhichao Xu, Kunpeng Liu

arXiv:2608.26019

DualOPSD turns a fixed privileged teacher into an adaptive teaching policy. The student first learns from privileged supervision; the teacher then moves toward the updated student distribution on the same trajectory, improving future supervision without another rollout.

On Qwen3-8B, DualOPSD improves avg@12 over OPSD by 23.61, 13.89, and 10.00 points on AIME 2024, AIME 2025, and HMMT 2025, respectively.
